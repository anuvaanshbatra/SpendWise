Questions, answered.

Who is SpendWise for?

Anyone whose money has to last: children learning the basics, teens, college students, working professionals, senior citizens, armed forces and All India Services officers, OCI cardholders and families relying on government schemes. Each gets a profile built around how they earn and spend.

Do I need to install anything?

No app store needed. SpendWise is a web app you open in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like a native app.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Core has no trial clock and no card required. Upgrade to Copilot only if you want automatic syncing and unlimited vaults.

Where is my data kept?

On the free plan, your data is stored locally on your device. Copilot adds automatic bank and UPI syncing, which you choose to switch on.
